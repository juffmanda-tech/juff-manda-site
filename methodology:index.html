How I work

The methodology.
Six stages.

Every engagement I run follows this framework. It’s not rigid—it scales to a three-week sprint or a twelve-month retainer—but the logic is always the same.

01

Audit & diagnose

Before strategy, understanding. I conduct platform audits, content analysis, competitive reviews, and stakeholder interviews to establish a clear picture of where things stand and why.

02

Strategy & architecture

Build the framework that governs everything downstream. Platform roles, content pillars, channel hierarchy, tone of voice, KPI structure. This is the document that everything else references.

03

Team & systems

Strategy is only as good as the team delivering it. I structure roles, build RACI frameworks, configure tools, and ensure every person knows exactly what they own.

04

Content & production

Platform-specific content frameworks. Brief templates. Production workflows. Quality gates. I build systems that make great content a repeatable output, not a lucky accident.

05

Community & listening

Community management as a strategic input, not a customer service function. Social listening that feeds back into content and strategy in real time, not just monthly reports.

06

Measure & optimise

Reporting that connects to business outcomes. Not vanity metrics—reach, engagement rate, share of voice, conversion contribution. Then iterate. Always.
